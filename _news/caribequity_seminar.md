I had the pleasure of presenting a virtual seminar titled "AI-Powered Business Growth: Using Generative AI for Startups & ESOs" for the CaribEquity Virtual Innovators Hub. This event was organized by IDB Lab, focusing on empowering startups and entrepreneur support organizations in the Caribbean region.

The seminar explored practical applications of generative AI technologies for emerging businesses and the organizations that support them, highlighting opportunities for innovation and growth in developing markets.
